How This Air Quality Project handles public website analytics, contact data and protected evidence boundaries.

Privacy policy

This Air Quality Project is operated as a public-interest evidence-screening website. The public site does not intentionally collect special-category personal data or publish protected evidence.

If Google Analytics is configured, aggregated usage information may be collected to understand page use. Cookie choices are stored locally in your browser.

Contact messages and corrections may be retained to respond to the issue raised. Do not send private medical, legal or confidential evidence unless an agreed secure route has been arranged.

How to read this page

Scientific evidence and software assurance are different

Scientific evidence

Measurements, station and regional coverage, descriptive calculations, source records and stated limitations are the evidence available for review.

Engineering assurance

Automated checks confirm that the pipeline followed its declared rules. They do not constitute peer review, accreditation, regulatory approval or institutional endorsement.

Evidence-screening intelligence for public-interest air-quality review.

Transparent acquisition, provenance, screening and uncertainty. No automated source-attribution, health or compliance finding.
