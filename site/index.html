AQ26 England Air Quality Observatory

This public repository has been established as the independent publication surface for AQ26. The first governed scientific data release is intentionally not published yet.

The private evidence engine has identified public-metric reconciliation corrections that must be completed and retested before the first public release. Withholding publication while those checks remain open is part of AQ26's scientific governance.

Scientific boundary

AQ26 is a descriptive evidence-screening observatory. No single monitor, model, satellite product, traffic record, permit document or other source establishes attribution, causation, individual exposure, health effect, permit breach or legal non-compliance.

Future releases will include explicit provenance, coverage, units, quality status, missingness and limitations alongside the evidence.

AQ26 is independent and is not an official Defra, Environment Agency, Met Office, Copernicus, NASA, National Highways or local-authority service.
