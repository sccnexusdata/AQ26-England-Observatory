REPRODUCIBLE RESEARCH CASE

17 July 2026 PM2.5 — fixed retrospective case

A frozen station-level evidence target for testing new AQ26 chemistry, meteorology, emissions, satellite and transport methods without moving the observations.

WHY THIS CASE IS RETAINED

A fixed observational target for method development

On 17 July 2026 the frozen dataset contains 121 valid PM2.5 station daily means. 80 were above the WHO 2021 PM2.5 24-hour guideline value used by AQ26 as a contextual reference, across all nine English regions.

The highest valid daily mean in the frozen set was 24.171 µg/m³ at Manchester Piccadilly.

This is useful scientifically because the observational target no longer changes. Future AQ26 methods can be compared against the same day rather than against a moving 30-day window.

NEXT EVIDENCE LAYERS

What will be tested against the frozen ground observations

Atmospheric composition

Specialist chemistry, Black Carbon, particle-number and ammonia evidence can test whether composition changes support or contradict competing explanations.

Meteorology and transport

Wind, boundary-layer context and trajectory analysis can test transport plausibility while remaining explicitly non-causal.

Emissions context

Spatial emissions inventories and point-source records can supply context, not proof of contribution.

Satellite and model context

Satellite columns and atmospheric-model fields will be labelled as different evidence classes and will not be represented as independent surface measurements.

How to read this page

Scientific evidence and software assurance are different

Scientific evidence

Measurements, station and regional coverage, descriptive calculations, source records and stated limitations are the evidence available for review.

Engineering assurance

Automated checks confirm that the pipeline followed its declared rules. They do not constitute peer review, accreditation, regulatory approval or institutional endorsement.

Evidence-screening intelligence for public-interest air-quality review.

Transparent acquisition, provenance, screening and uncertainty. No automated source-attribution, health or compliance finding.
