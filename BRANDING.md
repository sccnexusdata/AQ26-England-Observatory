# SCC Nexus affiliation

[![SCC Nexus](https://sccnexus.co.uk/assets/brand/scc-nexus-wordmark.webp)](https://sccnexus.co.uk/)

AQ26 / Air Quality England Observatory is an **SCC Nexus public-benefit evidence project**.

The Air Quality identity remains the primary project identity. SCC Nexus is the portfolio and governance affiliation: **Search · Corroborate · Communicate**.

Public-facing pages may use the SCC Nexus mark or wordmark in the header/footer affiliation area, browser metadata and project documentation where it does not obscure the project's own identity. Client-site branding rules do not apply here because AQ26 is an SCC Nexus public-benefit project.
