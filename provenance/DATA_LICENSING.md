# AQ26 public data licensing and attribution

## UK-AIR / Defra monitoring data

The England AURN evidence redistributed here is derived from official UK-AIR monitoring
information. AQ26 retains source identity, units, time basis, data-status flags and the
scientific limitations carried by the governed private release.

Attribution used by AQ26 for UK-AIR-derived material:

> © Crown 2026 copyright Defra via uk-air.defra.gov.uk, licensed under the Open Government Licence.

## Third-party evidence

The AQ26 private engine also tracks regulatory, meteorological, satellite, emissions,
traffic, commercial and research sources. Presence in the private source catalogue does
not automatically grant public redistribution rights. Raw or transformed third-party
records are added to the public allowlist only when their reuse basis is explicit.

## Scientific limitation

Permission to redistribute data does not establish source attribution, causation,
personal exposure, health impact, permit breach, operator fault or legal non-compliance.
